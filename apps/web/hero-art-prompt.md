Generated for Hyperlooms using the built-in image generation tool. The founder's supplied screenshot was a mood and composition reference, not an edit target. The resulting artwork is used only as a text-free background; page content remains semantic HTML.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-width website hero background for Hyperlooms, a serious community GPU compute network.
Input images: Image 1 is a mood and composition reference only, not an edit target. Borrow its black starfield, a huge shadowed planet and a radiant sun cresting the horizon; create an original scene, without recreating the reference's interface, text, logo, scan lines or glitch treatment.

Style: premium cinematic astronomy artwork, photorealistic lighting, physically plausible scale, sophisticated and restrained, high contrast, smooth black shadow areas, subtle film texture. Think important scientific discovery, expansive and hopeful, not a video game screen.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9. Put the bright sun around 42% from top; leave the upper quarter relatively dark and calm for navigation. Leave the bottom-center planet shadow almost black with ample negative space for an HTML headline and CTA overlay. Let the planet curve extend beyond both left and right edges. Full bleed edge-to-edge, no frame.
Palette: black and charcoal with warm ivory, copper, burnt orange sunrise, faint desaturated blue rim highlights. Preserve dark enough image regions for white readable website copy.
Constraints: absolutely no text, letters, interface panels, buttons, people, spaceships, logos, watermarks, grids, neon cyberpunk styling or duplicated planets. The output is background artwork only.
