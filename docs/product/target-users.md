# Target Users

## Consumer personas

### Student or independent ML developer

Needs occasional inference or fine-tuning capacity, has limited infrastructure knowledge, and values a guided workload experience over raw machine access.

### Open-source team

Needs repeatable batch jobs, transparent resource use, and a private pool donated by contributors.

### Small research group

Needs access policies, job history, reproducibility, and trusted membership before considering public capacity.

## Provider personas

### Individual GPU owner

Wants explicit schedules, temperature/resource safeguards, a kill switch, workload transparency, and evidence of earned value.

### Community lab

Wants to reserve shared machines for members, enforce recipes, and understand utilization.

## Initial wedge

The invitation-only pilot should target a single university or open-source Compute Circle. This constrains trust, improves feedback quality, and produces measurable evidence before public-network risks are accepted.
