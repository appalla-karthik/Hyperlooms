# Product Vision

## Purpose

Hyperlooms exists to make useful GPU compute accessible to people who cannot justify or afford equivalent hardware, while giving GPU owners controlled ways to contribute otherwise idle capacity.

The long-term product is a community-oriented compute network, not merely a machine-listing marketplace. Its differentiating direction combines trusted resource-sharing communities, reciprocal compute credits, understandable workload recipes, and evidence-based provider reputation.

## Product statement

> Hyperlooms is a community-owned compute fabric where trusted groups pool GPUs, earn reciprocal compute, and run verified workloads without needing cloud-infrastructure expertise.

## Initial users

- university AI and engineering clubs;
- independent ML developers;
- open-source maintainers;
- small research groups;
- friends or teams willing to share machines under explicit rules.

These groups provide a more concrete and safer initial environment than an anonymous global marketplace.

## Product pillars

### Compute Circles

Groups define membership, eligible nodes, approved workload recipes, scheduling priority, and credit rules. A circle can be private, organization-scoped, or eventually public.

### Reciprocal compute credits

Providers earn internal credits for completed, measured work and spend them on future workloads. Credits begin as closed-loop product units, not cryptocurrency and not cash-equivalent balances.

### Verified workload recipes

Users request outcomes through supported recipes rather than being given unrestricted shell access. A recipe declares its image digest, compatibility, resources, checkpoint behavior, network policy, inputs, outputs, and verification strategy.

### Compute Passport

Nodes build a factual execution record from successful jobs, benchmark history, availability, interruptions, verification results, and security events. Reputation complements technical controls; it never replaces isolation.

## Guiding question

Can a person with an ordinary laptop complete a useful AI task through Hyperlooms that previously required an expensive GPU workstation—simply, reliably, and at a sensible cost?
